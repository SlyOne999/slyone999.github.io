## Description ##
The Cardputer M5Stack with a LoRa add-on is a modular, industrial-grade development toolkit used by developers, researchers, and security auditors to test and exploit Long Range (LoRa) and LoRaWAN networks. Unlike the Flipper Zero—which focuses on short-range consumer wireless protocols—this combination targets long-range, low-power Internet of Things (IoT) infrastructure, smart city sensors, and industrial automation networks.

## Usage ##
Long-Range Interception: Captures radio signals over several kilometres, allowing audits from a safe, remote distance.Modular Processing: Features powerful ESP32-based microcontrollers capable of running complex script automations, cryptographic tools, and packet parsing.Network Interfacing: Bridges low-power LoRa networks directly to Wi-Fi or Bluetooth for live data extraction and remote command execution.

## LoRoWan ##
LoRaWAN is being used across the globe by major cities, massive corporations, and modern farms to automate services and cut costs.Here are six prominent, real-world examples of LoRaWAN in action:1. 🚰 Smart Water Management across France (Veolia)The French global utility giant Veolia uses LoRaWAN to operate over 5 million connected smart water meters across France. Instead of sending human workers to read meters outside homes, the meters text their water usage directly to the utility framework. This system immediately alerts the company to sudden drops in pressure or spike abnormalities, allowing them to locate and repair underground water pipe leaks before major wastage occurs.2. 🛣️ Highway Lighting in Greece (A1 Motorway)The A1 Motorway in Greece spans 550 kilometres, running through remote mountains and valleys where mobile phone signals are non-existent. To save electricity, the highway operators installed smart LED streetlights connected via LoRaWAN. Just 30 small antennas cover an entire 300-kilometre stretch of the highway, allowing the lights to be dimmed remotely or automatically turned up when a car approaches.3. 🏟️ Commercial Arenas & Air Monitoring (Atlanta State Farm Arena)In giant commercial buildings, installing miles of new ethernet cables to monitor the environment is incredibly expensive. At the State Farm NBA Arena in Atlanta, operators deployed peel-and-stick LoRaWAN sensors to continuously check air quality, temperature, and even monitor the fill rates of automatic soap and paper towel dispensers in restrooms. The batteries in these sensors last for years without needing maintenance.4. 🦏 Wildlife Conservation in Africa (Connected Conservation Foundation)In vast, remote African wildlife parks spanning thousands of square kilometres, cellular networks do not exist. The Connected Conservation Foundation deployed LoRaWAN networks across 35 wildlife parks in 10 different nations. Endangered rhinos and elephants are fitted with small tracking tags, and the park rangers track their locations in real-time to protect them from illegal poachers.5. 🏗️ Industrial Oil Refinery Maintenance (Shell Europe)Shell operates Europe’s largest oil refinery and uses thousands of mechanical valves that must remain strictly open or closed to prevent industrial disasters. Instead of manual inspections, they attached small, battery-operated LoRaWAN position sensors to the valves. If a valve accidentally turns or slips even slightly out of place, it sends an emergency alert over kilometres of steel and concrete structure directly to the control room.6. 🌽 Precision Agriculture on Corn Farms (Oregon, USA)An industrial corn farm in Oregon implemented a GAO Tek LoRaWAN-enabled precision agriculture system across thousands of acres of farmland. Hundreds of battery-powered sensors buried in the soil continuously measured moisture and nutrient levels. This live data allowed the farmers to trigger watering systems only when necessary, boosting their overall crop yield by 10% while saving thousands of gallons of water.
